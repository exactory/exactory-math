## 2. Hits worth reading

- Ferudun 2026, Sections 5-6 and the accompanying reports. The finitisation: after boundary
  coordinates are eliminated, every primitive outward rhombus normal is a nonzero 0/+-1 vector of
  squared norm at most four; for rank five there are 27 of them, 342 nonparallel pairs. A
  coefficient of t^{D-q} is localised on faces of codimension q, whose transverse cones are
  generated by q or more of those normals, so the coefficient's positivity is a finite check over
  the cone atlas at codimension q. Codimensions 0 and 1 are volume and surface; codimension 2 uses
  the closed formula alpha = 1/4 + (1/12)(C/A + C/B) with A, B, C the Gram entries of the two
  normals (Castillo-Liu Lemma 3.3), codimension 3 the closed formula
  alpha = 1/8 + (1/24) sum_{i<j} (G^{-1})_{ij} (1/(G^{-1})_{ii} + 1/(G^{-1})_{jj}) (Castillo-Liu
  Lemma 3.10) over an enumerated superset of 4,320 admissible Gram matrices with minimum 1/264,
  and codimension 4 at rank five the defining local Euler-Maclaurin recursion over all
  C(27, 4) = 17,550 four-normal tuples.
- The codimension-four report. 903 nonsaturated tuples with minimum weight 1/14400; 132 negative
  saturated cells, the extreme one being the tuple of normal identifiers (0, 7, 23, 25) with
  weight -66821/2858240; each negative cell is repaired by an exact slack-closure argument
  showing it either raises the normal rank to at least five or embeds in one of finitely many
  pointed rank-preserving closed supersets, every one of which has positive weight, minimum
  739/86400. This is the pattern any codimension-five attempt must expect.
- Castillo and Liu, arXiv:1509.07884. The same finitisation for permutohedra reaches dimension 6
  and codimension 3, which is where closed formulas for the weight stop.
- Rassart, arXiv:math/0308101. A second, independent finitisation at fixed rank: each coefficient
  is a polynomial of its degree on each chamber of the chamber complex of a vector partition
  function, so the linear coefficient is piecewise linear and its nonnegativity on a closed
  chamber is decided at the chamber's extreme rays.

## 3. What was learned, as constraints on the plan

1. Last value settled by computation, with encoder and solver: rank 4, every coefficient (the
   two-dimensional cone atlas of N_4, exact rational arithmetic, minimum weight 1/9); rank
   uniform, the top four coefficients of full-dimensional hives (codimension 2 and 3 atlases,
   the two closed formulas, minima 1/264 at codimension 3); rank 5, the coefficients of t^6 down
   to t^2 (the codimension-four atlas, 17,550 tuples, the local Euler-Maclaurin recursion with
   the slack-closure repair). The solver throughout is exact rational arithmetic on integral Gram
   matrices, not a polytope solver.
2. Independent checkers: the rank-four certificate and the rank-five codimension-two data are
   distributed with replay scripts (`proof/r4/q2_verify_r4_certificate.py`,
   `proof/r5/verify_r5_certificate.py`, `alpha5.py` with self-tests against the unit square and
   the unimodular triangle). The codimension-four computation is not: the report records only the
   SHA-256 of `r5_codim4_normal_subdivision_alpha_v2.py`, and that file is absent from the
   repository. So the one computation this attack most needs to extend cannot be replayed, and
   its method has to be rebuilt here and validated independently.
3. The residue in print and where it stops: Ferudun states it exactly. What remains at rank five
   is (i) the linear coefficient of full-dimensional five-part hives, that is codimension five,
   and (ii) the non-full-dimensional five-part hives of intrinsic dimension at least three, whose
   transverse cones are projections of N_5 and are "not covered by the atlas", together with the
   remark that these are not reduced to four parts by Horn factorisation (the example
   lam = (4,3,3,1), mu = (4,2,1,1), nu = (6,5,4,2,2) has dim H = 3 with all 142 essential Horn
   inequalities strict). Sizes are irrelevant to both: the atlas is rank-indexed, so a rung is a
   statement about all sizes.
4. The residue is per rank, and the number of rungs is infinite. This finitisation therefore does
   not finitise the claim; it makes each rung decidable. The strategy's first failure signal (no
   residue, the constants depend on the parameter) is the expected outcome for the claim itself,
   and the useful output is a rung.
5. The size of the rank-five codimension-five problem: C(27, 5) = 80,730 five-normal tuples, 4.6
   times the codimension-four count that was carried out, so the enumeration is within reach; the
   cost is the weight in dimension five, for which no closed formula exists in print.
6. Routes not to duplicate: the codimension-2 and codimension-3 atlases (in print, rank-uniform),
   the rank-four theorem, and the exhaustive enumeration of triples in a box (the box attack of
   this project, which settles sizes, not ranks).

## 4. Stop reason

Two readings of the only paper that finitises this statement and of its replay repository, plus
two searches confirming that the closed formulas for the weight stop at dimension three in the
literature as well. The last two sources add no further finitisation and no distributed
implementation of the weight above dimension three.
