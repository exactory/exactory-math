## Verdict

The conjecture is open. The first statement this attack aims at, the conjecture for every triple
of length at most five at every size, is open and is named as the immediate frontier by the only
paper that reaches the problem. The second, a nonnegative local formula for the rank-five
transverse cones, is the rank-five instance of (HTE) and is likewise open. The attack proceeds.
