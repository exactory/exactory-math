## What it does not mean, which is most of the finding

- It does not make the classical inequalities available. Stanley's theorem gives nonnegative h
  unconditionally for lattice polytopes, so every record whose hive polytope happens to be a
  lattice polytope carries no information, and this census cannot separate those from the
  genuinely rational ones without constructing the polytopes.
- It does not touch the conjecture. Reeve's tetrahedron has nonnegative h and a negative linear
  coefficient.
- What it does is remove the cheapest way the route could have died: one negative entry would have
  ended it and, by the contrapositive of root move 4, refuted the integer decomposition property
  for that polytope. None appeared.
