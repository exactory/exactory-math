## Exact statements added since the predecessor

1. Proved. Every edge `f` of a hive polytope lies on `c(f)` facets. Giving each facet containing
   `f` the amount `w(f) * len(f) / c(f)` distributes the whole of `w(f) * len(f)` and no more, so
   summed over the facets the shares are exactly the functional `l`. Hence `l >= 0` follows if
   every facet `F` satisfies

       sum over the negative-weight edges e on F of |w(e)| * len(e) / c(e)
           <=  sum over the positive-weight edges f on F of w(f) * len(f) / c(f).

   This mentions no cell, no chamber complex and no size of triple, and it is stronger than the
   neighbourhood statement of move 9 because a facet is smaller than the union of the facets
   through an edge.

   independent checks. Over the 2199 cells the project has built: 28,691 facets of which 2556
   carry a negative-weight edge and were therefore tested, 46,501 ray tests, no failure, and the

   tightest case is a facet of the hive polytope at (147770, 147594, 105002, 70327, 61221),
