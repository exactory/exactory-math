## Exact statement added since the predecessor

Proved, by derivation and with no computation in it. Let `H` be a five-part hive polytope in
`R^6` with `dim H = d < 6`, let `L` be the direction space of its affine hull and
`Lambda = Z^6 ∩ L`, a lattice of rank `d`. Since the Littlewood-Richardson coefficient is
positive there is `x0` in `H ∩ Z^6`, and for every positive integer `t`

    tH ∩ Z^6  =  t x0 + ( t (H - x0) ∩ Lambda ),

because a point `t x0 + z` with `z` in `L` is integral exactly when `z` lies in
`Z^6 ∩ L`. So the stretched polynomial is the Ehrhart function of the `d`-dimensional rational
polytope `H - x0` in the rank-`d` lattice `Lambda`. That is the intrinsic-lattice contraction
the record has owed since move 2.

## What this changes

Everything proved for the full-dimensional case is a statement about a polytope and its lattice
and uses no ambient rank, so the cell reduction, the nonnegative-class lemma, the discharging rule
and the per-facet identity all hold for the contracted pair. The atlas does not carry over: the
transverse cone of an edge lives in a quotient of `Lambda` and its normals are images of the
rhombus normals under the projection onto `L`. The work then divides by `d`, since the
transverse cone of an edge has dimension `d - 1`: `d` at most 2 is proved in the record by
reciprocity, `d = 3` needs only the closed pair formula, `d = 4` only the closed formula at
dimension three, and `d = 5` needs four-dimensional weights, one dimension below what the
full-dimensional case needed, for which `tools/bvweight.py` is built and validated.
