   implements Proposition 4.1 of Berline-Vergne (arXiv:math/0507256) as a recursion evaluated
   along a generic line in exact rational Laurent series, with saturations and quotient lattices
   by augmented Hermite reduction. Status: validated by `certified-special-case-check-2`
   (`result.json` pass) against the closed formulas of Castillo-Liu in dimensions two and three,
   the vertex-weight sum of the unit square, the valuation property on a non-unimodular cone, the
   orthants in dimensions four and five, and multiplicativity over orthogonal sums. A
   five-dimensional cone costs under a second.
2. The conversion from a hive face's normal set to its transverse cone in the projected lattice is
   correct: it reproduces the closed pair formula at codimension two, and the local formula with
   the computed weights reproduces the certified linear coefficients of hive polytopes exactly
   (89/30 and 13/4 on the first two tested, and five more inside the checker of item 3).

   cones that occur. Of the 3,406 isometry classes of the edge atlas, five have negative weight,
   the minimum being -347/109824 and the maximum over all classes 906853/3628800; each of the
   five is the full normal cone of an edge of an explicit five-part hive polytope of the box, and
   the checker reproduces each host polytope's certified linear coefficient from the atlas.
   Status: `enumeration-run-1`, `result.json` pass, with four independent checks.
4. Consequence for the rung: the argument that gives the top four coefficients of every
   full-dimensional hive, and the coefficient of t^2 at rank five, cannot be continued to the
   linear coefficient cell by cell with this local formula. Ferudun's negative cells at
   codimension four were not full normal cones of hive faces and were repaired by a slack
   closure; these five are, so no closure repairs them.
