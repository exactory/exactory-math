## Exact statements added since the predecessor

1. The cell functional can be read off the local formula. On a cell each edge keeps the facet
   normals through it, hence its transverse cone and its Berline-Vergne weight, while its lattice
   length is linear in the boundary triple, so `l(b)` is the sum over the edges of the class
   weight times the lattice length. `tools/atlasform.py` builds it, reading weights from the
   codimension-five atlas of `enumeration-run-1` and computing and caching a weight when a class
   is new. Nothing in this depends on the size of the triple.

   `result.json` pass. On every one of the 2006 cells of `certified-special-case-check-3` and at
   every one of their 9145 extreme rays, the value from the local formula equals the value that
   step obtained from finite differences of certified Ehrhart coefficients, and so does the
   certified coefficient at each cell's own triple. One computation counts lattice points in
   dilates; the other sums weights of transverse cones.
3. The size wall is gone. Status: `enumeration-run-2` phase B. 193 cells, every one of rank 14,
   built at triples of size 28,936 to 722,265 with a median of 221,438; 4764 extreme rays
