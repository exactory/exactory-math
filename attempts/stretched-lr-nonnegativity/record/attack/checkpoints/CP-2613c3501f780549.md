## Exact statements added since the predecessor

1. The rung reduces to a statement with no size in it. Write a triple as `b` and the hive
   polytope as `H(b) = {x : A x + C b >= 0}`, `A` the thirty rhombus rows in the six interior
   entries and `C` their boundary parts. The cell through a triple `b0` with `H(b0)` full
   dimensional is the set of `b` at which every row tight at a vertex of `H(b0)` stays tight and
   every row slack at a vertex stays satisfied, cut by the dominance inequalities and
   `|lam| = |mu| + |nu|`. It is a rational cone holding `b0` in its relative interior, and there
   are finitely many cells, a cell being the finite datum of which rows are tight at which
   vertex. On the relative interior the polytope keeps its face lattice, every vertex is a fixed
   linear function of `b`, every edge keeps its transverse cone and has a lattice length linear
   in `b`, so the local Euler-Maclaurin formula makes `a1` a linear functional; the same follows
   from Rassart's piecewise polynomiality (arXiv:math/0308101) with the homogeneity
   `a_k(m b) = m^k a_k(b)`. Hence `a1 >= 0` on a whole cell holds exactly when the functional is
   nonnegative at the cell's extreme rays.
2. The certificates. Status: `certified-special-case-check-3`, `result.json` pass, with four
   independent checks. 2006 cells, 1347 distinct, of rank 3 to 8 and one of rank 14; 9145 extreme
   rays evaluated; none negative; minimum 0, maximum 143/15; 7109 ray values exactly zero. Every
   cell reproduces the certified coefficient of the box triple it was built at, every cell's own
   triple is a nonnegative combination of its rays, and all 6018 spot checks at random interior
   lattice points agree.
3. Consequence: the linear coefficient is nonnegative on 1347 unbounded cones, not only at the
   2000 certified triples inside them. This is the first statement in this project about triples
   of unbounded size that is not a sampling result. Rank one would have been no gain, homogeneity
   already covering the ray through a certified triple. One cell is full dimensional: at the
   triple (109,74,72,63,55), (61,57,25,17,5), (77,52,42,23,14) the cell has rank 14, 321 facets
   and 16 extreme rays, and the functional is nonnegative on all of them, so the coefficient is
   nonnegative on a fourteen-dimensional unbounded cone of the Littlewood-Richardson cone.
4. Where the method stops, recorded rather than assumed: 24,571 further sampled triples gave only
   that one full-dimensional cell. 19,112 were too large for the certified Ehrhart run, 3,605 gave
   a hive polytope that is not full dimensional, 1,674 lay on a wall, 178 had too many vertices.
   The wall is the cost of the Ehrhart computation, which grows with the size of the triple, while
   a generic triple small enough to be cheap is almost always on a wall.
