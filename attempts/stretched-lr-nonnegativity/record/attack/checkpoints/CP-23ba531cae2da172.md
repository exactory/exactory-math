## Exact statements at this checkpoint

   <= 30, every coefficient of P(t) = c^{t lam}_{t mu, t nu} is nonnegative. Status: proved by
   computation, every one of the 5,552,431 polynomials computed by two programs sharing no code
   (Normaliz on the hive inequalities; lrcalc with exact interpolation), every enumeration's
   completeness recounted independently for every size.
2. No triple of length at most 7 and size at most 30 has a negative coefficient, so the
   FrontierMath open problem has no solution inside its box. Status: computational evidence at
   7 parts (1,302,871 primitive polynomials by two encodings in one solver, matched by lrcalc at
   t = 1 and 2; 9,201,781 non-primitive triples reduced by the King-Tollu-Toumazet factorisation
   theorem to statement 1).
3. Of the 6,855,302 computed polynomials of the box, exactly 7,469 have a coefficient below the
   corresponding coefficient of the unimodular simplex; every one of them has exactly D + 1
   lattice points, normalized volume 2 and h*-vector (1, 0, 1, 0, ...), in dimensions 4, 5 and 6
   with minimal ratios 24/25, 44/45 and 799/812. Status: a census computed in two
   implementations that agree (`certified-special-case-check-4`, `result.json` pass).
