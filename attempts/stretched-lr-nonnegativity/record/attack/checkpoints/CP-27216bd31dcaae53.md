## What is not claimed

Ten forms are a finite sample of a twenty-one-parameter continuum, so nothing says no form lifts
all twenty-one. Only the standard form and `A_6` have passed the codimension-six vertex-sum gate of
`enumeration-run-4`, and the two best forms are not among them. Lifting a representative is not
lifting its class, because a non-standard form breaks the collapse by signed permutations and the
classes would have to be replaced by types. A form lifting all twenty-one would still need the
other 5,121 held classes re-evaluated under it, about an hour, outside the allowance.
