# Novelty record

Statement searched: "for all integer partitions lam, mu, nu with at most 5 parts each and
|lam| = |mu| + |nu|, every coefficient of c^{t lam}_{t mu, t nu} is nonnegative" (the rank-five
rung), together with the two statements this attack expects to reach: nonnegativity of a McMullen
local weight on the five-dimensional transverse cones of rank-five hive edges, and the
determination of such weights from recorded Ehrhart data.

## Queries (source, string, date, outcome)

1. The parent attack's novelty record (`../ktt-positivity-conjecture/novelty.md`, queries 1-5 of
   2026-09-05, which include the inherited arXiv, Semantic Scholar, zbMATH, Crossref,
   MathOverflow, web and repository searches of 2026-09-03 and 2026-09-04): no proof of the
   conjecture, no result for length five, and no counterexample.
2. arXiv (MCP search), `hive polytope Knutson Tao Ehrhart polynomial stretched coefficients
   positivity proof`, 2026-09-05: 8 hits; the only rank-five material is Ferudun 2026, which
   names the linear coefficient of full-dimensional five-part hives and the non-full-dimensional
   five-part hives as open.
3. arXiv (MCP search), `Ehrhart positivity survey techniques Berline Vergne local formula Liu`,
   full abstracts, 2026-09-05: the closed weight formulas stop at dimension three (Castillo-Liu
   reach dimension 6 and codimension 3 for permutohedra), so no source computes the weight this
   rung needs.
4. zbMATH and Crossref, inherited from the parent and the finished attacks (2026-09-03 to
   2026-09-05): no rank-five positivity result.

## Neighbouring statements searched

- Rank four, every coefficient: solved (true), Ferudun 2026.
- Rank five, coefficients of t^6 to t^2 of a full-dimensional hive: solved (true), the same paper.
- Rank five, the linear coefficient: open, named as open there.
- Rank five, non-full-dimensional hives of intrinsic dimension at least three: open, explicitly
  not claimed there.
- Determination of McMullen local weights from Ehrhart data of a family: no source found. The
  literature computes weights from a construction (Berline-Vergne, or the lattice-tile
  construction of Ring and Schuermann) and then evaluates them; the inverse direction, pinning
  the weights by the recorded Ehrhart polynomials of the family they apply to, is not in print.

## Verdict

The rung is open, both of its parts are named as open by the only paper that reaches them, and
the route this attack takes to it is not in print. The attack proceeds.
