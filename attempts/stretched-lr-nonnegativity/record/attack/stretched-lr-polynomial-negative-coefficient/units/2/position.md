# Position note, unit 2

On the path: the unit is the answer to the target itself. The FrontierMath open problem
"Stretched Littlewood-Richardson coefficients" (Epoch AI) asks for partitions of length <= 7
and size <= 30 whose stretched Littlewood-Richardson polynomial has a negative coefficient; the
unit states that no such partitions exist, as a proof by computation for at most 6 parts (unit
1) and as computational evidence for 7 parts (one solver, two encodings, lrcalc at t = 1, 2).

Prior best result: Ferudun 2026 (length <= 4, all sizes, a theorem; extensive uncertified
computation without ranges); De Loera and McAllister 2006 (LattE computations at small rank,
no negative coefficient); the ktt-positivity repository (a ~4 x 10^8-triple search, ranges
unrecorded). This unit records the box exhaustively with the certification status of each part
and the structural minima (the 24/25 ratio at the volume-2 empty simplices).

Venue: the problem's own page (a progress note answering the open problem negatively inside
its box), and a computational note or arXiv preprint carrying the problem-paper content: the
state of the attack, the ceilings (no second Ehrhart solver; no translation reaching the box),
and the open sub-questions (size 31; 8 parts; whether 24/25 is the infimum of the ratio over
all hives; whether empty hive simplices of normalized volume 3 or more exist).
