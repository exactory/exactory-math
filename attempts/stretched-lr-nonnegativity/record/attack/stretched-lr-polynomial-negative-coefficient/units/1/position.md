# Position note, unit 1

On the path to the target. The target of this attack is the box (length <= 7, size <= 30) of
the FrontierMath open problem; the unit settles the part of the box with at most 6 parts, as a
theorem proved by computation with two independent programs and an independently recounted
enumeration.

Prior best result it extends: Ferudun 2026 (arXiv:2607.22301, Theorem 1) proves positivity for
every triple with at most 4 parts, for all sizes; the same paper reports "extensive computation"
with no counterexample and its repository a search of about 4 x 10^8 triples, both without
recorded ranges or certification. This unit is a different kind of statement: finite (a box),
exhaustive, and certified (every polynomial by two programs, every size's completeness
recounted). It does not improve the theorem for all sizes.

Venue: a short computational note (the certified box at 5 and 6 parts, with the structural
minima), or an appendix to the box evidence of unit 2.
