- Step 3: c^{kappa+(1^n)}_{rho+(1^n), sigma} = c^{kappa}_{rho sigma} for partitions with at most n parts (V_kappa tensor det in GL_n; the GL_n multiplicity equals the symmetric-function one when every partition has at most n parts). For lam and mu with exactly n parts, t lam and t mu have n-th part >= t, so t columns come off and the triple (lam - 1^n, mu - 1^n, nu) has the same P, at most 6 parts, and size |lam| - n with |mu - 1^n| + |nu| = |lam| - n. The induction on |lam| is well founded. Sound.
- Step 4: P is the Ehrhart quasi-polynomial of the rational polytope H and a polynomial, so Ehrhart-Macdonald reciprocity reads P°(t) = (-1)^{dim H} P(-t). dim 2: P(t) - P(-t) = 2 a_1 t = P(t) - P°(t) = boundary lattice points of tH >= 0, a_2 = relative area > 0, a_0 = P(0) = 1. dim 1: 2 a_0 = P(t) - P°(t) >= 0 and 2 a_1 t = P(t) + P°(t) >= 0. dim 0: P = 1. Sound.
