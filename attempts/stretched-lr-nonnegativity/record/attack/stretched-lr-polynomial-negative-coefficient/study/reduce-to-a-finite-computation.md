## 2. Hits worth reading

- KTT 2006 (full text). The hive model with the three rhombus types and the edge-label form of the conditions; Definition 4.1 (primitive: every essential Horn inequality strict) and Conjectures 4.2-4.3 (a saturated essential Horn inequality factorises c and P as products); Proposition 5.1, deg P <= d(G), the number of interior connected components of the skeletal graph not touching the boundary, Conjecture 5.2 (equality exactly when primitive); Section 6: P has the factor (t+1)(t+2)...(t+M) with M the last dilation whose interior has no lattice point (Ehrhart reciprocity), and Conjecture 6.2 that for simple triples (every Horn inequality strict) P(-m) equals a signed negatively-stretched LR coefficient. Two computed examples in the box or near it: lam = (4,3,3,2,1), mu = (4,3,2,2,1), nu = (7,4,4,4,3,2,1) (7 parts, |nu| = 25) has c = 13 and P = (t+1)(t+2)(t+3)(t+4)(t+5)(5t+21)(t^2+2t+4)/10080, degree 8; lam = (7,6,5,4), mu = (7,7,7,4), nu = (12,8,8,7,6,4,2) has c = 12 and P = (t+1)(t+2)(t+3)(3t^2+7t+5)/30. Their computations used the program of Nzeutchap-Toumazet-Butelle (distributed computation of Kostka numbers and LR coefficients, cited as [NTB], to appear in 2006). No ranges are stated.
- De Loera-McAllister 2006 (abstract, and the ktt-positivity report's citation of its Conjecture 4.7). Lattice-point counting with LattE for tensor product multiplicities at fixed rank; tables of computed examples; positivity restated as a conjecture. Only the abstract was read; the paper's tables were not fetched.
- Ferudun 2026 and its repository (full text of the paper and of the listed files). Engine A: a C++ depth-first hive enumerator with interval propagation; Engine B: a Python lattice-word LR-tableau counter with memoisation; calibrated against each other on all triples with |nu| <= 8 and against c = 1, 2 stretched values, then against lrcalc. Interpolation: exact Newton interpolation through L(0..D) with L(D+1), L(D+2) held out; a mismatch is a degree anomaly, never a hit. The negativity hunt ("waves"): waves 1-2 sampled triples with the enumerators (about 5 minutes per stretched count at 6 parts, per the `hive_poly.py` header); wave 3 planned a triage oracle giving dim H and the maximal vertex denominator without counting, to pre-select the dimension-9/10 (6-part) stratum and to flag h*-prefix negativity (c <= dim H) as the non-integral region "waves 1-2 never entered". The report states about 4 x 10^8 triples searched with no hit, ranges unrecorded. The audit records the largest vertex denominator at 5 parts as 7.
- Coquereaux-Zuber 2018 (zbMATH record only): hive polytopes and the SU(4) linear coefficient, the "alleged" positivity Ferudun cites; not read.
- Epoch background PDF (problem-level study): the verifier "computes the polynomial and checks if some coefficient is < 0" from the hive polytope; degree bound d <= C(l+1, 2); "hard going past l(lam) >= 6 or so".

## 3. What was learned, as constraints on the plan

1. The last values settled by computation: every triple of length <= 4 (theorem, not computation); about 4 x 10^8 triples with no recorded range (Ferudun's hunt); the hunt's cost model was enumeration (minutes per polynomial at 6 parts), so its coverage of 6 and 7 parts was thin, and its wave-3 non-integral stratum (c <= dim H) is where it had not yet gone as of 2026-07-22. This attack's encoder (`tools/hivepoly.py`, the same hive model as KTT 2006 Definition 3.1 and Engine A) with Normaliz computes a polynomial in 0.01 s at dimension <= 6 and 0.1 s at dimension 11, so the 6- and 7-part strata are the place to go.
2. Every prior certificate had an independent checker: two counters plus held-out interpolation. This attack keeps that discipline: Normaliz's polynomial is the encoder's result; the independent check is the depth-first hive count at t = 0..D+2 with exact Newton interpolation (no Normaliz code), plus lrcalc at t = 1 (the classical rule, no hive code).
3. Structure to exploit: a saturated essential Horn inequality factorises P (KTT Conjecture 4.3, proved for essential facets per Ferudun's "Horn factorization" citation of KTT 2009), so a counterexample can be sought among primitive triples; the leading factor (t+1)...(t+M) is fixed by the first dilation with an interior lattice point, so the negative coefficient must come from the cofactor Q(t) = P(t)/((t+1)...(t+M)), which is small in degree when M is large; deg P = dim H is computed exactly by Normaliz.
4. Within the box, generic 7-part hives are not full-dimensional: 678,000 random 7-part triples with |lam| in 24..30 produced no hive of dimension above 11 (`tools/speedtest-dim15.log`), so the box's strata are dimensions 3..11 and the search is over them.
5. Routes not to duplicate: the 3 x 8 transportation transfer (dead); random low-size sampling at 5 parts (covered by the prior hunt's scale).

## 4. Stop reason

One query per tier-one source was run (two returned empty from Semantic Scholar's search endpoint, one was refused by arXiv's rate limit; their targets were reached through the citation list and the repository). The last two sources (the KTT 2006 full text and the repository engine notes) added the degree/linear-factor structure and the hunt's cost model, and nothing after them changed the plan.
