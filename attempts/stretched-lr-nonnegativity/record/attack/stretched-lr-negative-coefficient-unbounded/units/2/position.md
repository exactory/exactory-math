# Position note, unit 2

On the path: the conditions are what any search for a counterexample to the King-Tollu-Toumazet
conjecture can assume, and they define the classes unit 1 sampled.

Prior results assembled: the factorisation theorem (King, Tollu, Toumazet 2009; Cho, Jung, Moon
2008), the c <= 2 theorems (Knutson, Tao, Woodward 2004; Ikenmeyer 2016; Sherman 2017), the
length <= 4 and full-dimensional 5-part theorems (Ferudun 2026), Rassart's piecewise
polynomiality (2004), and the box computation of the sibling attack. The conjunction and the
chamber consequence are not stated in print; each ingredient is.

Venue: a section of the problem paper (with unit 1 and the box units), not a standalone paper.
