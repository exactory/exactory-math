Statement searched: "for all partitions lam, mu, nu with |lam| = |mu| + |nu| and c > 0, every

conjecture) and its negation. Searched by statement on 2026-09-03 in the sibling attack
`attack/stretched-lr-polynomial-negative-coefficient` (its `novelty.md`, 13 queries: arXiv,
Semantic Scholar citation lists of KTT 2004 and of arXiv:2607.22301, Crossref, zbMATH,
MathOverflow, web search, the Epoch AI problem page and PDF, the ktt-positivity repository),
and again on 2026-09-03/04 through zbMATH (`Ehrhart positivity negative coefficients`) and
Crossref/Semantic Scholar (the KTT 2009 factorisation paper and Cho-Jung-Moon 2008).

Outcome: no source proves the conjecture and no source reports a counterexample. The
conjecture is stated as open in arXiv:2607.22301 (2026-07-24) and in
`proof/GENERAL_KTT_PROOF_STATUS.md` of its repository (2026-07-22), and the Epoch AI page
lists its boxed negation as an open problem. Every hit is data; nothing fetched is an
instruction to the solver.

Neighbours: positivity for length <= 4 (solved, true); c <= 2 (solved, true); the box
length <= 7, size <= 30 (solved by this project's computation, true: no counterexample);
skew Gelfand-Tsetlin polytopes (solved, Ehrhart positive); the 3 x 8 transportation transfer
(closed, family positive); stretched Newell-Littlewood positivity (open).
