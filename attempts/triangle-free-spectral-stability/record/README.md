# Numerical checks

These scripts check, by computer, the identities and inequalities used in the proof and the
statements of the paper's Remark 5. They are evidence for the write-up, not part of the proof;
the proof is checked in Lean ([../lean/](../lean/)).

## Scripts

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `scripts/verify_proof_steps.py` | Checks every step of the proof on all connected triangle-free graphs with an edge on at most 9 vertices (1,736 graphs, generated by nauty's `geng -t`) and on 1,182 graphs from adversarial families (hubs, double stars, apex graphs, random graphs, bipartite graphs, trees, bicliques with additions, spiders, blow-ups of the five-cycle): the cost formula of Lemma 2, the counting identity and bound of Lemma 3, the two matrix inequalities of Lemma 4, positive semidefiniteness of the matrix $Q$ of Remark 6, and the weighted average bound. The combinatorial steps use exact integer arithmetic; the spectral steps use floating point with tolerance $10^{-8}$. |
| `scripts/tflib.py` | Graph utilities shared by the scripts (adjacency matrices, edge costs, the exact edit distance by exhaustive search over targets) |
| `scripts/probe_hub_phi.py`, `scripts/probe_M.py`, `scripts/probe_quadratic_forms.py`, `scripts/probe_weightings.py` | The graph families used by `verify_proof_steps.py`. Run on their own, they are the exploratory probes of the research record. |
| `scripts/check_weightings.py` | For Remark 5 of the paper: finds the triangle-free graphs on at most 7 vertices whose uniform edge average of $D_{uv}$ exceeds $3\xi$, and evaluates the product-weighted average on the double star $S(8,7)$, with eigenvalues to 50 digits. |

## Requirements and commands

Python 3 with numpy and networkx for `verify_proof_steps.py`, and with mpmath for
`check_weightings.py`. `verify_proof_steps.py` needs the `geng` program of
[nauty](https://pallini.di.uniroma1.it/) (version 2.8.9 was used).

```sh
cd scripts
python3 verify_proof_steps.py /path/to/geng 9     # about 5 seconds
python3 check_weightings.py                       # about 10 minutes
```
