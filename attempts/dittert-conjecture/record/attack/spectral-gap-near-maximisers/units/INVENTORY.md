## ladder-the-parameter

- move 2 (pass 1, make-the-bound-explicit-then-attack-the-lossiest-step, paid bound_quality): The identities hold exactly (maximum error 4.4e-16 at n = 8) and mu = phi is confirmed. Two structural facts entered the record. First, the minor of the Knopp-Sinkhorn extremal through its zero is exactly van der Waerden extremal: per(T_n(0|0)) equals ((n-2)/(n-1))^{n-1} gamma_{n-1} to every printed digit at n = 5, 6, 7, 8, since that minor is the uniform (n-1) x (n-1) matrix; the problem has a recursion in n that no route uses yet. Second, T_n is not a stationary point of phi on K_n: its gradient at the zero exceeds mu by 9.9e-3 at n = 5 and 1.0e-3 at n = 7, so moving mass into the zero raises phi, which is the conjecture's content at the extremal. The rung itself is weak: the minor bound's right side is at least gamma_n - delta - 2 sqrt(2 delta), positive only for delta < gamma_n^2/8, so it is silent in the regime delta ~ gamma_n where the difficulty sits, while the matching upper bound per(M) <= (s_M/(n-1))^{n-1} ~ 1/e is far above gamma_n. Bound quality is what the rung gives up: it constrains a minor rather than the spectrum, and only in a range of delta that does not contain the hard case.
- move 3 (pass 1, choose-the-auxiliary-weight-or-certificate, paid bound_quality and obligations): New lemma, proved and checked: for A with a Sinkhorn-connected support, |alpha_i + beta_j| <= sqrt(R_eff(i,j) Phi) <= sqrt(Phi / a_ij) wherever a_ij > 0, with Phi = (u;v)^T L^+ (u;v). Consequence: condition (N) holds automatically at every entry with a_ij >= Phi, with no spectral hypothesis at that entry. The bar this replaces c_N with is lambda_2 >= 2 delta kappa_n/(theta_n a_min): linear in delta where the old one was sqrt(delta), but now carrying 1/a_min instead of nothing. Taking the heuristic a_min ~ 1/n, the new bar is 0.105 at n = 7, 7.6e-3 at n = 10, 1.3e-3 at n = 12 and 9.0e-5 at n = 15, against the old c_N of 0.183, 0.0395, 0.0149 and 3.5e-3: a gain of 1.7x at n = 7 rising to 38x at n = 15. Combined with the refined Cheeger-and-blocks bound lambda_2 >= 1/(8 n^2), this would settle n >= 13 and leave 7 to 12. Two obligations remain and are recorded in study/removing-condition-N.md: a lower bound on the entries of a near-maximiser (none is in print), and the margin repair after deleting the entries below Phi, whose cost is second order but whose connectivity is not guaranteed by Cheon-Wanless Thm 3.2. Bound quality is what the rung pays: the new condition is weaker in delta but is now entrywise.
- move 4 (pass 1, optimise-the-certificate-family-numerically, failure signal fired): Two negative results, both decisive for the rung. First, the entrywise lower bound is false on the natural relaxation: the smallest positive entry reaches 3.6e-28 at n = 6 and 9.3e-29 at n = 7 while per stays at 1.05 gamma_n, against the 1/n of the Knopp-Sinkhorn extremal; only n = 5 (2.9e-2, ratio 0.14) and n = 8 (5.6e-4, ratio 0.004) stayed away from zero, and those are search failures rather than obstructions. Second, the truncation repair is quantitatively impossible, not merely awkward: truncating the entries below Phi moves the margins by at most sum (a_ij + sqrt(a_ij Phi)) <= n^{1.5} sqrt(Phi) + n^2 Phi, and since Phi >= 2 delta always, that error exceeds the original margin size sqrt(delta) by a factor of at least n^{1.5} sqrt(2). So the repair enlarges what it is meant to fix. The rung of move 3 therefore does not climb: the effective-resistance lemma is true and new, but on its own it does not remove condition (N). What survives into the record is the lemma itself, the exact statement of what it would need (an entrywise lower bound, now refuted on the relaxation), and the reason the obvious repair fails. Also recorded as a methodological limit: the constraint set of this claim is conjecturally empty, so every numerical test is a test of a relaxation, and a relaxation that is non-empty is one in which the structure being sought can be absent.

## reduce-and-translate

- move 1 (pass 1, change-the-ambient-space, paid obligations): The decisive finding is that Theorem R's binding constraint is technical, not mathematical. Theorem R needs lambda_2 to clear both the nonnegativity condition (N) of its Lemma 2 (bar c_N = 2 sqrt(gamma_n kappa_n)/theta_n) and the final contradiction (bar c_C = 2 m_n kappa_n/theta_n). The ratio c_N/c_C = 1/sqrt(gamma_n kappa_n) is 12.1 at n = 7, 51.7 at n = 10, 576.8 at n = 15 and 6555 at n = 20, so (N) is binding everywhere on the open range, by up to three orders of magnitude. The structural reason: the margins u = 1 - r have size sqrt(delta), so the linear correction alpha has size sqrt(delta)/lambda_2 and nonnegativity costs a square root, while the contradiction is linear in delta. Consequence for the routes: against c_C the crude Cheeger-and-blocks chain (which proves lambda_2 >= 1/(2 n^4)) settles n >= 15, and with a block capacity bound exp(-C eps) in place of (1 - eps)^n it settles n >= 10 for C = 2 and n >= 12 for C = 4; against c_N it settles nothing below n = 35. The rubric was corrected accordingly. Also recorded: G(n, c_n) and Dittert's conjecture at n are equivalent, not one weaker than the other, since Theorem R gives one direction and the conjecture makes G's hypothesis set empty. The obligation the move leaves is a competitor B' that is nonnegative and doubly stochastic by construction, which is what removing (N) requires.

## strengthen-and-generalise

- move 9 (pass 2, strengthen-the-target, failure signal fired, paid implication): The local constant of S(n, c) is exactly n/(2(n-1)): with E of zero row and column sums, per(J_n/n + E) = gamma_n (1 + (n/(2(n-1))) ||E||_F^2) + O(E^3), because the first-order term vanishes (per(A(i|j)) is constant at J_n/n and sum E = 0) and sum_{i != k, j != l} E_ij E_kl = ||E||_F^2. Verified numerically to a relative error below 4e-6 at n = 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 (0.666667, 0.625000, 0.600000, 0.583333, 0.571429). The strengthening is then worth nothing: ||T_n - J_n/n||_F^2 = 1/(n-1)^2 exactly, so S with its true constant predicts per(T_n)/gamma_n - 1 of order n/(2(n-1)^3), which is 1.62e-2 at n = 7 against the true 1.34e-2, the same quantity to leading order. So the candidate strengthening reproduces Knopp-Sinkhorn and adds nothing, and the failure signal of the strategy fires on its first candidate: the deduction to the original does not cost a factor, it simply does not gain one. What this settles for the whole attack is that the permanent side is already as strong as it can be, both at J_n/n (exact Hessian) and at the boundary (Knopp-Sinkhorn), and the single open interface is the capacity bound of move 8. Implication is what the move pays: only the direction from S to the claim is proved, and it turns out to be uninformative.

## work-conditionally-then-discharge

- move 5 (pass 1, condition-on-the-one-missing-input, paid obligations): The conditional theorem with its threshold table: K_max(n) = 0.179, 0.301, 0.536, 1.00, 1.94, 3.91, 8.09, 17.2, 37.2, 82.0, 184, 417 at n = 4 to 15, and 2.9e4 at n = 20. So H(n, K) with a universal K closes every n with K_max(n) > K; K < 1 already settles n >= 7, K < 8 settles n >= 10, K < 400 settles n >= 15. Measured against the truth: the sibling attack's capacity samples give K about 0.5, so the hypothesis has a factor of two of room at n = 7, the hardest open dimension, and orders of magnitude above it. This is the cleanest form the problem has taken in either attack: it is a statement about matrix scaling alone, with no permanent, no spectrum and no simplex in it, and Theorem R is the special case K = 1/(2 theta_n lambda_2). The obligation is H itself; what its proof needs is the second-order behaviour of the Sinkhorn fixed point without a spectral gap, or a convexity argument on the scaling functional that avoids its Hessian.
- move 6 (pass 1, condition-on-the-one-missing-input, failure signal fired): H(n, K) with a universal K over all fully indecomposable A is false. For a B that is block diagonal with two doubly stochastic blocks, the linear map eps -> (u; v) has a kernel of dimension equal to the number of blocks (scale the rows of one block up and its columns down), while the second-order scaling cost sum_b k_b t_b^2 on that kernel is strictly positive; so the ratio is unbounded and K = infinity for a decomposable A, and arbitrarily large for a nearly decomposable one. The hypothesis therefore has to be restricted, and the restriction that does the work is precisely that A is not nearly decomposable, which is what per(A) <= gamma_n has to supply. That closes the circle the attack has been walking and states its one open link exactly: a dichotomy. Either cap(A) >= gamma_n/m_n, in which case per(A) >= cap(A) m_n >= gamma_n contradicts per(A) = gamma_n - delta with delta > 0 and the case is closed; or -log cap(A) > log(m_n/gamma_n) ~ 1/(2(n-1)^2), which says A is a definite distance from doubly stochastic, and in that branch a block or expansion lower bound on per(A) has to take over. Route R1 of study/routes-rubric.md is the only candidate for that branch and it was computed at move 1 to be too lossy below n = 35 against the (N) bar, and below n = 15 against the contradiction bar. What the attack still lacks is a lower bound on per(A) that is good exactly when cap(A) is small; every bound in the record degrades in that regime.
