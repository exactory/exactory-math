We formalize a finite scalar layer of an existing computer-assisted argument for Dittert’s conjecture. For dimensions 11 through 15, the artifact checks direct rational formulas for every ordered proper cut and one normalized marginal cut, then derives a strict polynomial inequality for every real parameter u ≤ 1. Its corollary excludes simultaneous permanent and deficit lower bounds even under a relaxed scalar budget. Lean verifies 340 normalized cases and proves that they cover the entire specified nonfull scalar domain. Independent execution and statement review confirm that the positivity, exclusion, and finite-certificate declarations depend only on propext, Classical.choice, and Quot.sound. A separate appendix verifies two literal near-zero polynomial certificates in dimensions 8 and 9 on specified closed intervals; it does not establish full scalar coverage in either dimension. The contribution is an attributed partial formalization and reproducible verification artifact. The matrix reduction, its analytic and structural inputs, and the full Dittert theorem are outside the formalization.
