R={B^a:a in A}, where B>=2, A is finite and sigma(R)>=1/2, align the exponents
at a common multiple L. The normalized labelled weights satisfy the exact
signed-prefix interval criterion and represent all integers. Thus R labels
above any cutoff represent every multiple of B^L. Other bases give a disjoint
modular correction to1. This covers all such common-radix families, including

Any counterexample consequently has no base3, has power-of-two subfamily mass
below one half and satisfies the remaining stable-tail restrictions. These
