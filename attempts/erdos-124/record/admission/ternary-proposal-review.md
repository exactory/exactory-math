## Mathematical substance

The proposal identifies a concrete change of mechanism. The predecessor obtains a minimum-three threshold of 13/4 through allocation and finite correction. The new plan restricts to a set containing three, uses the balanced ternary digit alphabet for a tail unit construction on two bases, and leaves all other bases available as a separate reserve. This is sufficient mathematical specificity for admission to a proof attempt.

The arithmetic prerequisites match the stated domain. Because three belongs to D and gcd(D)=1, some b in D is not divisible by three, hence gcd(3,b)=1 and b>=4. The proposal does not require all bases to be pairwise coprime. Its reserve mass calculation is correct:

    sigma(D minus {3,b}) = sigma(D) - 1/2 - 1/(b-1)
                        >= 11/6 - 1/2 - 1/3 = 1.

The saved ordered-deficit argument explicitly permits mass equal to one. It treats coincident numerical values as different labels, handles every positive cutoff, and absorbs finite deletions into a finite gap constant. It requires no gcd-one condition on the reserve itself. Thus equality at 11/6 does not introduce a missing strict inequality in this interface.

The proposed use of congruences and balanced ternary has the right structural ingredients for the missing seed. A base coprime to three is a unit modulo each power of three, and the signed digit alphabet permits at most one occurrence of each ternary exponent on each side after signs are separated. The actual argument must still establish arbitrarily high legal support, a sufficiently high exponent for the b label, finite positive alternatives differing by one, and compatibility with every forbidden finite set. The displayed background theorem establishes classical balanced ternary only. It is not evidence that this complete tail construction has already been proved or formally checked.

The iteration also has a substantive requirement: the two alternatives of every new gadget must avoid the union of all labels used by earlier gadgets. Avoiding only one chosen representation would not establish the proposed interval growth. The studies explicitly require fresh disjoint copies beyond arbitrary cutoffs and track finite supports, so this obligation is present rather than silently assumed away.

The independent reserve addresses the earlier failure for a precise reason. Its gap constant can be fixed from its own bases, k, and the reserve deletions before any seed support is chosen. Building a longer seed then consumes no reserve label and cannot enlarge that constant. The saved Section 1 interface asks for one finite interval with the required number of consecutive sums. The proposal therefore does not infer cofinite coverage from thickness alone or from an abstract signed-unit property. It names the additional disjoint reserve needed for the implication. This assessment admits that plan; it does not execute the seed construction or prove a new theorem.
