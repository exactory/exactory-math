# Novelty preflight: pure-power central seeds

Date: 2026-09-07.

Exact target: for every admissible finite D,k,F, there is a single integer
L>=0 such that arbitrarily late labelled prefixes have all their central
integer coefficients, from L through S_m-L, represented and S_m>=2L.

Every query and response is listed in sources/queries.md. The five first-tier
sources were queried for the problem and for the planned strategy. Semantic
Scholar returned rate-limit errors, which are preserved as failures rather
than successful searches. Focused web searches supplied Fan 2026 and a primary
discussion of Cassels's method. No exact solution was identified.

Known results are not new claims: geometric-sum identities, finite generating
function coefficient extraction, conditional interval propagation, Cassels's
criterion under its stronger growth condition, and the disjoint-component
completeness criteria of Bergelson-Simmons and Fan. The target may retain
essentially all the original difficulty; translating it is not a theorem of
independent significance.

The root's positive-cutoff quantifiers were checked against the saved formal
statement Erdos124.erdos124.ne_zero and the BEGL96 conjecture. The solved
zero-cutoff result, the k=1 example D={3,4,7}, and cutoff-dependent finite
subsets of an infinite base set do not close this task.

The work is not publication-ready and has no novelty claim at this stage.
A prospective nontrivial result needs fresh statement-specific searches and
independent validity review before it can be accepted or made into a unit.
