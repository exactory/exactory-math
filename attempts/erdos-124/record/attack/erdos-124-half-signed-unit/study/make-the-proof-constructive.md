# Strategy study: normalize signed supports beyond an arbitrary cutoff

## Queries and source reads

QUERY_LOG.md supplies the five tier-one constructive-strategy queries and
the exact read boundaries. The relevant primary context is the classical
signed-weight prefix criterion in Bolker-Feuer-Zara and the accepted
self-contained ternary and binary tail-unit constructions. No published

## Value to beat and planned branch

The target is exact unit difference for every admissible E,k,F, not an
extremal constant optimization. The smallest positive signed difference
is one. The seed branch is selected; extremal-candidate step 2 is not
applicable. At most two seed-and-amplify applications may use the same
remaining account. No finite mathematical execution is proposed.

## Proposed constructive question

Start by specifying an exact extension or normalization operation on
finite signed supports and proving its preservation law before claiming
a seed construction. The candidate approach is to organize the signed
prefix sumsets and their least defects, using pure-power shifts and
integer coefficient normalization to try to remove the forbidden low
exponents. Investigate whether the signed prefix surplus at mass one half
and collective gcd one force a legal unit without coefficients outside
{-1,0,1}. Every subtraction and union must track overlap of original labels.

The actual tail-normalization operation is not established. This is an
explicitly uncertain precondition to test, not an operation assumed valid
because there is a finite search procedure. A zero-cutoff signed interval
only supplies a comparison model. A successful all-tail construction must
give its own finite termination or strictly decreasing invariant while
keeping all exponents beyond the chosen cutoff. Its size may be unbounded
as a function of that cutoff.

If an operation is proved, determine its smallest valid motif and apply
it analytically, preserving both signs and the finite-deletion exclusions.
No computer search, numerical no-hit prefix or finite modular coverage
may substitute for this proof.

## Distinction from previous attempts and failure signals

The earlier carry attack sought a large positive seed with a quantitative
residual support ledger on the same supply. This new task asks only for a
signed tail unit and leaves positive amplification to a wholly disjoint
reserve. Its input family begins at reciprocal mass one half. It is not
a renamed copy of that carry claim or the refuted generic profile claim.

Failure includes inability to define a gain-producing legal normalization,
a step requiring a repeated identical label or unavailable coefficient,
a cutoff-dependent preamble that cannot be removed, or nontermination.
If the bounded attempts do not resolve the initialization, record exactly
that missing lemma and retreat under the admitted criterion. A failure of
this route is not a counterexample to the half-mass claim or the root.

The source study's fixed count is reached. The claim, amplification and
normalization are all unproved at this preflight stage.
