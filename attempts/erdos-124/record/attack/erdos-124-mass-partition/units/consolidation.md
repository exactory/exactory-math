## Rewritten proof with each role visible

1. A g-term interval plus a disjoint g-syndetic reserve covers a tail
   of the nonnegative integers. This is the final amplification.
2. Bounded ordered deficit gives bounded gaps for labelled subset sums,
   even after finite deletion. This constructs the reserve property.
3. The established sumset theorem makes the sums from two bounded-gap
   supplies piecewise Bohr. Full multiplier divergence on the correction
   supply gives density in the relevant compact orbit group.
4. Compactness chooses finite correction support. Trimming the thick
   set gives arbitrarily long represented intervals. What remains of
   the correction supply is still bounded-gap.
5. The accepted reciprocal core preserves the minimum and gcd one.
   A first-crossing second group gives the three-way mass partition.
6. Exact geometric sums give each power supply's deficit bound.
   The integer nearest-point recurrence and collective gcd give full
   multiplier divergence on the correction supply.
7. The resulting finite-seed search and representation search terminate
   by these analytical lemmas. No numerical search was needed or run.

## Second route and general statement

No independent second proof is claimed. The method shares the
piecewise-Bohr input and finite-correction method of Bergelson-Simmons
and Fan. Treating those inputs as a different route would misstate
independence.

The generic statement actually used is the occurrence-preserving
three-component criterion in section 3. A useful broader sufficient
condition for the pure-power application is simply that D admit three
base groups each of reciprocal mass at least one, with collective gcd
one in the correction group. The displayed mass threshold constructs
such a partition uniformly. The proof does not claim its necessity
or optimality, or a criterion for the complementary root range.

## Disposition and source boundary

This is an internal proof candidate and exact application of established
methods, not a claimed publication-level discovery. The numerical-set
engine and the inherited core are not new in this attack. The detailed
label audit is essential for correctness but does not itself establish
publication novelty. CASHOUT.md therefore does not justify a standalone
paper unit merely from this corollary. Independent checkpoint review
remains mandatory before the high-mass obligation can be accepted.
