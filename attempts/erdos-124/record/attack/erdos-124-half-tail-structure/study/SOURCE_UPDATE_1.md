Date: September 8, 2026. This does not replace the admitted strategy study.
The exact queries were: `"signed sums" "powers" "complete sequences"`;
`"weakly complete" "powers" sequence`; and
`"4" "5" "powers" "signed" "Erdős"`. The complete returned response is saved in
preflight/half-signed-unit/sources/halfTailSourceDiscoveryA.json.
No exact all-base half-mass theorem was identified. The terminology search was
noisy and mostly returned unrelated topics. An author-owned P283 formalization
plan on GitHub discusses Graham's polynomial infrastructure, not this pure-power
claim. It is not a proof dependency here. No external theorem from these results
is used in the working lemmas, which are proved directly in the move file.

A second bounded discovery used `"powers" "complete sequences" "Graham"`,
`"sums" "powers of 4" "powers of 5" integers`, and
`"signed" "completeness" "powers" "Cassels"`. Its complete response is saved
as halfTailSourceDiscoveryB.json in the same sources directory. It recovered
Burr, Erdos, Graham and Li, Complete sequences of sets of integer powers,
Acta Arithmetica77(2),1996, DOI10.4064/aa-77-2-133-138, and unrelated fixed-degree
power completeness results. The displayed first-page excerpt of the 1996
paper restates the original conjecture, not the new signed-tail lemma.
No additional primary theorem is imported from this second discovery.

The aligned common-radix argument is a direct development of the classical
signed-prefix interval criterion and the already studied local binary
construction. Its novelty and full successor claim must be studied separately
before declaring an accepted result. These six queries exhaust this supplement.
