## Burr, Erdos, Graham and Li (1996)

Read the complete existing local text extraction sources/begl1996.txt
relative to the research root. The paper distinguishes a fixed finite base
set from finite subsets whose choice depends on the exponent cutoff.
Theorem1 starts with an infinite positive-density base supply; its proof
uses a bounded-gap reserve, repeated arithmetic-progression identities on
new bases, and residue representatives. Theorems2 and3 concern consecutive
base ranges, with the latter's range depending on the cutoff. The reported
small finite sets are handled at cutoff1. None of these statements removes
the cutoff quantifier in the present arbitrary finite-base claim.
This reread does not provide a half-mass signed theorem.
