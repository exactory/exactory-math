## What was learned and selected plan

No published exact counterexample or all-base half-mass signed tail theorem
was identified. There is no recorded computationally searched range for this
claim. The accepted high-mass positive theorem excludes counterexamples in
that higher range: consecutive representations yield a signed unit after
cancelling common labels. The special common-radix constructions also supply
known positive configurations. These are the necessary conditions used to
justify entry into the negative-side strategy; they are not a finite reduction.

The proposed constructive opening has an unknown gain-producing operation.
Its seed-and-amplify operation-existence trigger is not certified by proposal
approval. Do not dispatch it on that unknown. The fresh native scan admits
the negative-side strategy, which can instead extract arithmetic conditions
from a hypothetical counterexample without assuming that operation exists.

Plan step3 is selected because the prior information is a list of necessary
conditions, not a resource curve or an available guessing principle. The
entry is extract-structure-from-a-hypothetical-counterexample. It will assume
a missing unit at an admissible input and investigate the integers representable
in every sufficiently high tail, the effect of disjoint supports, and the
distinction between stable integer obstructions and cutoff-dependent gaps.
Any group structure or equivalence encountered must be proved in the reserved
move; none is asserted by this study. Accumulate conditions only as far as
their hypotheses are justified.

A finite range, if found, is not permission to run a program. The analytical
admission has computation:null. No finite or random search is planned.
The full claim and all its quantifiers stay fixed. A necessary-condition
list or reformulation leaves the claim open unless it supplies a contradiction
or a verified counterexample. At most two local analytical applications are
planned before assessing this route against its failure signal and account.
