The question was whether a theorem about syndetic finite-sum sets already
provides the proposed one-class piecewise-Bohr input. No such theorem was found.

Primary material read:

- Griesmer, arXiv0911.2278, https://arxiv.org/html/0911.2278,
  Section2.5, especially lines310-320 in the saved response. It explicitly
  gives a syndetic set defined by a quadratic irrational phase condition
  that is not piecewise Bohr. This establishes the distinction for arbitrary
  syndetic sets. It is not a counterexample whose set is proved to be the
  finite-sum set of one of our pure-power supplies.
- The same source's Lemma2.9 and Corollary2.10 concern cutting a fixed Bohr
  set into long intervals and translating the pieces. The compact phase
  subsequence keeps a nonempty open set. Those results do not themselves
  produce such pieces inside a single finite-sum set.
- Adams, Hindman and Strauss, Largeness of the set of finite products in a
  semigroup, Semigroup Forum76 (2008),276-296, DOI10.1007/s00233-007-9006-8.
  Author PDF https://nhindman.us/research/largefp.pdf was found. Its direct
  open failed. Search-indexed primary excerpts include the abstract and
  some Section3 results. The abstract concerns nice sequences and states
  an equivalence involving syndeticity, centrality, piecewise syndeticity,
  and a bounded prefix deficit. The definition of nice and the full
  Section4 theorem have not been read. No Bohr conclusion was identified,
  and no theorem from this paper is imported.
- The Tao-Vu book search excerpt from its proof of Theorem12.17 needs
  both a separate progression-producing supply and a bounded-gap supply.
  The complete theorem and its square-root density hypothesis were not
  read from the book. The sequence density and finite-sum density must
  not be confused. No general subcompleteness claim is imported.
- The BBF author PDF open was attempted with the second search but no
  usable new theorem was read. Its known two-positive-density sumset
  theorem remains background.
