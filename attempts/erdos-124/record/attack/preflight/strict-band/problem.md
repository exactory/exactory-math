## Prospective exact scope

For every finite set D of distinct integer bases d>=3 with gcd(D)=1 and
sigma(D)=sum_{d in D}1/(d-1)>1, every positive cutoff k, and every finite
labelled deletion F, there exists one integer L>=0 such that arbitrarily late
remaining labelled prefixes cover every integer in [L,S_m-L], with S_m>=2L.

This is the strict-mass part of obligation-000008. There is no positive lower
bound imposed on sigma(D)-1. The equality case and the full original objective
remain open. No native branch was created and no root obligation was narrowed.
