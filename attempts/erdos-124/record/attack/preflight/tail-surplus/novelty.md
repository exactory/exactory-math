# Novelty assessment

Exact claim: For every finite set D of distinct integers at least 3 with gcd(D)=1 and sigma(D)=sum_{d in D}1/(d-1)>=1, every integer k>=1, and every finite set F of labelled occurrences (d,j) with d in D and j>=k, list the remaining occurrences by increasing value and then increasing base label as a_1,a_2,... and put S_m=sum_{i=1}^m a_i; then S_m-a_{m+1} tends to positive infinity as m tends to infinity.

Date: 2026-09-07. Query strings, source access, every API hit, selected web hits and identifiers are recorded in sources/queries.md and its saved responses. See problem.md and reduce-and-translate.md for the primary text inspected.

The limited search found no exact all-D, all-positive-cutoff, all-finite-deletion surplus theorem. It did find classical finite-rank equation finiteness and the closely related use of power-gap bounds in BEGL96. Accordingly, a successful proof is expected to be an auxiliary corollary of established tools. It is not claimed to be independent publication novelty or a solution of Erdos 124.

The neighbouring zero-cutoff completeness theorem is known; the positive-cutoff objective and matched seed are unresolved in the inspected sources. Unrestricted pairs of equal powers cannot satisfy a universal nonzero-gap divergence statement. The proof must handle this obstruction from the actual input hypotheses rather than assuming multiplicative independence of every pair.

No result is declared in this study.
