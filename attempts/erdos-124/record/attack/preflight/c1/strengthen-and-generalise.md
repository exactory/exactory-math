## Constraints selecting the first candidate

Root move 1 isolates the exact operation: a central interval with endpoint
loss L extends by a new occurrence a when a <= S_m-2L+1. The operation itself
is classical. The admitted root study and this successor's primary-source
review rule out using bounded gaps, gcd one, or residue coverage alone as an
already proved seed theorem.

The first strategy step will formulate a seed-plus-surplus strengthening of
C1. Its constants must survive finite deletion, and its implication must use
only disjoint new occurrences. It must separately expose, not assume, the
initial interval and the condition at every later scale. Strict reciprocal
mass and equality may behave differently; neither case is excluded from C1.

Small hand checks may test the sufficiency and sharpness of the proposed
mechanism. They cannot refute eventual coverage merely by finding a small
missing integer, nor support it by a no-hit finite range. No mathematical
process, solver, or expanding enumeration is admitted by this proof proposal.

The strategy is a logical deepening of the recorded C1 checkpoint, not a new
root attempt or a budget reset. A meaningful strict-mass lemma or different
claim will receive its own reviewed successor before substantive development.
