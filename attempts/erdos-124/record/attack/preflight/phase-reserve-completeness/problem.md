## 1. Exact question and search record

For every finite nonempty set D of distinct integers at least four with
gcd(D)=1 and sigma(D)=sum_d 1/(d-1)>1, every positive cutoff k and finite
labelled deletion F, can the remaining labels be divided into disjoint
infinite supplies T,R with FS_0(T) thick and FS_0(R) having one fixed
finite consecutive-gap bound? In particular, is there a source-backed
extension operation that extracts finite interval witnesses into T while
protecting R and controlling the deleted weight at every scale?

The complete [query log](QUERY_LOG.md) records twenty exact queries and
links every returned hit in five raw result batches. Queries1--5 address
the problem and queries6--10 address the proposed constructive strategy
across the five tier-one source indexes. The remaining ten query the
neighbours, prior complete-sequence methods and support-cost question.
The native APIs were not used in this follow-up. Indexed Crossref and
Semantic Scholar results had low recall; the results are not an
exhaustive bibliographic certification.

The canonical local [Lean statement](../../../sources/124.lean),
`Erdos124.erdos124.ne_zero`, was reread in full. It retains all finite D
with d>=3, collective gcd1, sigma>=1, every positive cutoff and eventual
coverage of every integer. The proposed strict-mass minimum-four
partition would cover only that domain. Minimum3 and reciprocal equality
remain original obligations, not exceptions silently removed from it.

## 3. Constraints on the next plan

The primary methods confirm that disjoint support is a mathematical
hypothesis. A finite shift from FS(C), a dense set of numerical subset
sums, or a represented interval is not an automatically affordable set
of occurrence labels. The proof of CP26 gives a fixed-gap complement
after individually selected near-aligned powers, not after arbitrary
finite interval-support blocks. Its thickness conclusion consumes that
complement. The full phase proof was reread in this preflight and gives
no unused second reserve or interval-support weight estimate.

The new Conlon--Fox--Pham lead identifies real modular-growth and
interval-extension tools, but their input conditions have not been
verified here for legal fixed-base blocks. Merely renaming the previous
node8 switch ledger or applying Lemma2.1 after an uncontrolled witness
would leave exactly the retained failure unresolved.

No source inspected supplies a concrete all-scale extension operation
for the exact proper-partition hypothesis. This is a failure to locate
an applicable operation within a bounded preflight, not a proof that the
partition is false, not a universal method barrier, and not a novelty
certification. No analytical move, mathematical program, reservation,
account renewal or new theorem candidate was produced.
