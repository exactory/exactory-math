## 7. Spectral transfer and the exact reserve boundary

CP25 uses the sequence of finite Bernoulli subset-sum measures. Square-character divergence gives the needed decay for that sequence. The proof integrates against finite Radon spectral measures using dominated convergence before passing to a mean. This is the full Fourier-Stieltjes-mean condition needed by Griesmer, not just annihilation of individual nontrivial characters by an arbitrary finitely additive mean.

That distinction is substantive. In the inspected Griesmer text, Definition3.4 imposes the unitary-coefficient condition, Lemma9.5 gives the spectral-measure characterization, and Lemma10.3 with Remark10.4 explains the sequential passage. Question10.5 prevents treating an arbitrary character-annihilating mean as an automatic substitute. The new plan retains the correct sequential interface.

Corollary4.2(ii) applies to `A=FS_0(R)` and `B=FS_0(Q)` with positive invariant mass for A and full FS-mean mass for B. The label-disjoint partition then identifies their sumset with `FS_0(Q union R)`. The consequence is thickness of that full supply. It is not thickness of `FS_0(Q)` alone.

R has already been used in that sumset. It cannot simultaneously be treated as an additional untouched reserve for a final completeness step. The older positive proofs explicitly fixed a disjoint reserve before building intervals, and the conditional WOC bridge obtains finite corrections on one supply before fixing its remaining reserve gap. Those are different support interfaces. Neither the present claim nor this review establishes a proper thick supply plus another bounded-gap reserve.
