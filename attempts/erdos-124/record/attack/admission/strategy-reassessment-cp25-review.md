## Assessment of the proposed phase preflight

The proposed question is useful and sufficiently focused for a bounded source preflight. CP25 identifies a concrete place where its lower bound uses more mass than the proposed strict-mass domain provides. Actual geometric prefix sums depend on relative power phases; studying those phases directly targets that estimate. The proposed question also asks for one cyclic selection using every base with bounded exponent gaps, so it has a specific spectral purpose. It is not merely another constant improvement in CP24's completed partition.

This justification does not establish the extension. When `sigma(D minus {h})<1`, CP25's displayed lower bound has a coefficient below1 on x. Its positive buffer alone does not restore the proof for all later scales. A phase recurrence statement must therefore be connected to the actual retained-prefix inequality, with all earlier removed weight charged. Calling an alignment favorable is not that connection.

The draft and manual comparison appropriately require the following outputs before proposing new execution:

1. An exact phase system and a relevant primary recurrence result, with its hypotheses checked on the actual logarithms. Collective gcd one is not multiplicative independence, and recurrence must be considered on the appropriate orbit closure rather than assumed on a full torus.
2. A concrete next-power operation with an invariant bounding complement gaps at all scales, including between favorable returns and after every previous selection. The gap must be fixed before extraction. If near-aligned selections occur in groups, their combined deletion cost also belongs in that invariant.
3. Return control strong enough to give bounded selected exponent gaps for every base used. Mere infinitely many returns does not supply that bound. Any phase tolerance or return bound must work throughout the recursion for each fixed input.
4. Legal labels after every cutoff and finite deletion, one Q independent of the character, and a precise scope and bridge for any narrower successful family. Equality and minimum-three cases remain outside the proposed strict-mass minimum-four preflight.

These are checks of the proposed success and failure criteria, not new proved lemmas or instructions to perform a mathematical move during this review. The absence of a finished operation is not a blocker to this assessment because it continues no strategy and approves only investigating whether the operation can be made concrete. The source preflight has a fixed20-query ceiling and the required tier-one source floor. It follows submission of this assessment. Failure to find a lawful operation ends this proposed path at that evidential scope; it creates no automatic reservation, new account or identical retry.

The stronger previous hypothesis is expressly retained: a proper thick supply plus a separate syndetic reserve. In CP25, `FS_0(Q)+FS_0(R)=FS_0(P)` is thick, but its interval witnesses may consume R.
