The proposal changes the support allocation in a precise way. CP22 allocates all labels of a correcting base set C to signed-unit construction and requires reciprocal mass at least one on the remaining bases. The new proposal allocates only individually selected positive correction labels from V. Each must satisfy the fixed near-alignment test and the threshold above Lambda(C+W) used in CP26, as well as the proposed exponent-period condition for the current modulus. All other labels of its base remain available to the reserve. Fresh common-radix labels supply the signed multiple left after that correction. Here C in the threshold denotes the prefix-deficit constant, not CP22's correcting base set.

This is a concrete operation with explicit constraints, a proposed invariant and identifiable failure signals. It targets the difficulty that stopped node8: a signed unit of uncontrolled support does not keep the residual subset-sum gap small enough. CP26 supplies a scale-sensitive one-label estimate that was unavailable to that failed attempt. The new operation proposes to enforce that estimate at each deletion, before constructing a completed gadget. The existence of every required extension remains unknown. That unknown is correctly recorded as a strategy precondition and proof obligation, rather than treated as an established consequence of the studies.

The amplification must retain the same g from V,k,F before the first unit is chosen. It must use a cumulative W for all positive V corrections already selected, including corrections belonging to earlier units.
