## Proposed route and limits

Open with make-the-proof-constructive, moving undecided mode to
construction. Its seed branch is selected by a proposed finite first
positive buffer, not by cost. The wanted object is the actual infinite
Q,R partition in the claim. Extending a finite selected prefix by a
least eligible power increases its number of selected labels while
aiming to preserve a fixed gap bound and a bounded adjacent-value ratio.
The seed-and-amplify entry must prove these properties, not merely
state a recurrence. It must check the strict buffer at the first seed,
iterate through all H, quantify exponent gaps, and derive the exact
spectral condition. The resulting interval witness search is described
as a procedure only; its termination depends on the cited qualitative
sumset theorem, with no complexity bound claimed.

Plan step 2 is inapplicable because the target quantity is the explicit
partition and interval witnesses, not an extremal constant. The first
application is step 3, seed-and-amplify. No finite process or seed
enumeration is authorized. The procedure is the mathematical object
to prove, not a covert executed computation.

Relevant quantities: number of selected labels; total removed weight;
the fixed complement gap; and maximum exponent gap in each H class.
The proposed construction pays no weakened statement or added
hypothesis. Any unpaid amplification lemma remains an obligation,
and a closing claim is impossible until it is proved.

Failure signals: the recursion does not preserve the complement gap;
the buffer becomes nonpositive; selected exponent gaps become unbounded;
the collective gcd argument does not give every nontrivial character;
or the full FS-mean transfer loses once-only labels. A method failure
does not refute the original problem.
