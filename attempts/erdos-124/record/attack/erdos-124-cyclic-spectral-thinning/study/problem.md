## Constraints learned

The candidate must remove individual labels while retaining a fixed
gap bound on the complement. It cannot charge the whole removed support
against a finite unit gadget and hope for an unspecified later surplus:
that was the support-cost obstruction of node-000008.
It also cannot remove arbitrarily sparse powers without an upper bound
on the exponent gaps, since that would not justify the spectral input.
A proof must explain rational as well as irrational characters and the
collective gcd, preserve finite deletions and numerical coincidences,
and take sequential dominated limits before any invariant-mean cluster
point, as in CP24.

The candidate hypothesis is a least-power recursion cycling through H.
Its proposed invariant compares the value of the latest removed power
to the total previously removed weight and the fixed cutoff/deletion
constant. No invariant or thickness theorem is proved in this study.
Construction and the Griesmer transfer require reserved mathematical work.

## Neighbours and contribution

The uniform sufficient condition sigma(D)>=1+1/(min D-1) would imply
H=D if the claimed construction works. This is a consequence to audit,
not an extra accepted theorem. The known common-radix half-mass signed
unit theorem covers some inputs outside this criterion, and is not
subsumed. General half-mass signed units, the below-criterion remainder,
minimum-three cases and original completeness all remain open.

A proposed route to the existing half-mass signed-unit obligation will
retain its complementary input range and an explicit bridge: an interval
of two consecutive legal sums gives a signed unit by cancelling common
labels. The separate root routes and their reserve obligations remain.
No equality case or conditional premise is silently dropped.
