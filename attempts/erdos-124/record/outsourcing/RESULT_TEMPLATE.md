## Verdict

## Argument

Give a self-contained proof, or identify the first unjustified step and explain
why it is not justified. Separate proved lemmas from conjectural steps.
For a counterexample, check every hypothesis and the failure of the conclusion.

## References and evidence

For each imported theorem, give its exact source identifier, theorem location,
hypotheses, and application. Distinguish inspected sources from remembered leads.
If authorized computations were performed separately, preserve the input domain,
code, actual output, exit status, checker, and limits. Do not describe examples
as a proof of an infinite statement.

## Scope and contribution

Explain exactly what this establishes for the requested task and what it does
not establish for the original all-D, all-positive-cutoff objective.
Assess mathematical validity separately from publication novelty.
Identify the exact root obligation or parameter range that would be discharged
if the result were accepted. Do not assign a proof-completion percentage to an
unverified candidate, a conditional outline, or the amount of work performed.
