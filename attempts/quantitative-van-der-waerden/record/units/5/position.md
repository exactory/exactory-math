# Position of unit 5

**On the path or off to the side.** On the path of the application, Dittert conjecture, which is
why the claim was attacked. It is a structure theorem about counterexamples rather than a proof.

**Prior best it improves.** Theorem R of the sibling attack reduces Dittert conjecture at n to a
spectral hypothesis on the counterexample, with the binding constraint carried by lambda_2(A) of
the counterexample itself. This unit replaces that hypothesis by a theorem in one of its two
branches: with unit 2 and Lemma 2', every counterexample at n >= 8 has a scaling cost of at least
K_2(n) and a Sinkhorn scaling with gap at most 1 - sqrt(2 n K_2(n)). The comparison between
lambda_2(A) and lambda_2(B), which Theorem R needed and which was measured but not proved, never
arises, because Lemma 2' is stated with lambda_2(B) from the start.

**What it does not do, and the correction it records.** An earlier note in the record claimed
that the deduction closes n >= 8. It does not: the fixed-point inequality K <= Psi(K) has two
solution branches (study/branch-structure.py), and only the small-cost branch is excluded. The
large-cost branch, where the scaling is near-decomposable, is the barrier of unit 6.
