# Position of unit 2

**On the path or off to the side.** On the path: it is the claim of the attack with the constant
1/(2n) in place of 1/4, proved for every n. The claim itself is tight at n = 3 with 1/4 and the
measured sharp constant rises to 1/2, so 1/(2n) is a factor of about n/2 short of the truth.

**Prior best it improves.** The van der Waerden inequality itself (Egorychev 1981, Falikman
1981), which is the case constant 0. No quantitative version with a term in the distance to
J_n/n was found in print.

**What it gives the application.** With Lemma 2' (unit 5) it excludes every counterexample to
Dittert conjecture at n >= 8 whose Sinkhorn scaling has a spectral gap bounded away from zero.
It does not exclude the near-decomposable branch (unit 6), and it does not reach n = 7, which
needs the constant 1/n.
