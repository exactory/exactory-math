# Position of unit 4

**On the path or off to the side.** On the path, as the evidence that fixed the claim. The
constant 1/4 in the claim is the measured minimum over n of c*(n), and the extremal family
(I + C_n)/2 at n = 3, 4, 5 is what any proof has to be tight against.

**Prior best it improves.** No search over the Birkhoff polytope for this ratio exists in print.
The two explicit constructions in the literature, J_n/n and T_n, both give 1/2 in the limit.

**What it is not.** A multi-start local optimiser gives an upper bound on the true minimum, so a
value above 1/4 is evidence that no counterexample exists under the schedule the run fixes, not
a proof. The claim at n = 3 is proved by unit 3; at n >= 4 the evidence is this unit and the
proved constant is 1/(2n) (unit 2).
