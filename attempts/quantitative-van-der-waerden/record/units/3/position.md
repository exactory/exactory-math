# Position of unit 3

**On the path or off to the side.** On the path, at the one dimension where the claim is sharp:
c*(3) = 1/4 exactly, attained at (I + C_3)/2, so the claim cannot be improved at n = 3 and this
unit proves it there with equality cases.

**Prior best it improves.** The plain n = 3 case of van der Waerden is classical; the quadratic
deficiency term at n = 3 was not in print. The proof is two lines once the identity is written:
the difference between the two sides is a sum over rows of the degree-one Schur expression.

**What it does not do.** The identity is special to n = 3: at n >= 4 the analogous row sums do
not reproduce the deficiency, and the measured minimisers leave the family (I + C_n)/2 at n = 6.
The local theorem of the same route gives Q(1/n) only within an explicit radius of the centre.
