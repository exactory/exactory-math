# Two Eigenvalues

## Problem

Study the graphs forced by the Nosal threshold and give a simpler proof of the triangle-free two-largest-eigenvalues inequality.

## Claims

Theorem 2 proves a booksize lower bound m^(1/4)/12 above the edge spectral threshold, except for complete bipartite graphs with isolates. Theorem 4 states lambda_1^2+lambda_2^2<=m for triangle-free graphs of order at least three, with equality families given by blow-ups of P2, 2P2, P4 and P5 with isolates.

## Assumptions

Finite simple undirected graphs. The second eigenvalue is the second largest algebraically, not the least eigenvalue in absolute value. Connectedness and nonbipartiteness are imposed only where explicitly needed in intermediate booksize arguments.

## Methods

The booksize proof removes edges with small Perron coordinate product and applies weighted walk and triangle estimates. The proof of Theorem 4 uses a convexity lemma comparing p-th power sums under a square-sum bound, together with trace(A^3)=6t(G). The equality classification invokes the cited classification of graphs with two positive eigenvalues.

## Limitations

There is no complete-bipartite edit-distance theorem here. The equality-classification dependency is cited rather than independently reproved. The source uses k>=3 in Lemma 10; applying the theorem at very small graph orders requires the elementary small-order cases, not an unmentioned application outside that lemma.

## Relevance

This is a primary comparator for spectral-moment methods and the relationship between the first two positive eigenvalues. It does not settle the selected coefficient-three edit inequality or the proposed signed-cut linear estimate.
